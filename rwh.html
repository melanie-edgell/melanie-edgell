Rainwater Harvester System Model

My APSC 101 team and I created a spreadsheet simulation that modelled the use of a rainwater harvester (RWH) system over the course of 5 years of rainfall. We developed a plan suitable for a community experiencing water scarcity to get sufficient potable water every day–an alternative to shipping bottled water. We prepared a presentation to summarize our findings and present our recommendation.

My responsibility was ensuring the spreadsheet simulation functioned correctly. It begins with a ‘Parameters’ page where I am able to change aspects of our RWH design and see the impact on stakeholder satisfaction. This involves analyzing the energy usage, system curve pressure of the water pump, greenhouse gas emissions, geographical layout, and applying it to rainfall data. I created formulas to integrate all of these components into the simulation to accurately reflect the system’s performance and stakeholder satisfaction.

I significantly developed my spreadsheet competency with this project. I am able to incorporate complicated formulas into a cohesive spreadsheet simulation. These skills can be applied to data analysis in my future engineering projects.
