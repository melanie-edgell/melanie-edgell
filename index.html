About Me

My name is Mel and I am a second year UBC Engineering Physics student. I have experience designing and implementing control systems, embedded electronics, and electromechanical prototypes through academic projects. I have strong technical problem-solving skills, and have demonstrated the ability to adapt quickly to challenging situations, learn new technologies, and contribute to project success. I am able to communicate complex ideas clearly and collaborate well within multidisciplinary teams to bring projects from concept to reality.

I am most interested in projects that focus on coding, hands-on building, and sustainability. I am also passionate about investigating how we can use AI to streamline engineering projects. I hope to use my engineering skills and passion for continuous learning to make a positive impact on our society.
