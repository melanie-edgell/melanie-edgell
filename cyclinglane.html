Cycling Lane City Development Plan

My APSC 100 group and I analyzed 5 prospective locations for a cycling lane in Vancouver. We took in stakeholder input and performed a weighted decision matrix to analyze our options.

In particular, I worked on the justification for our weighted decision matrix. This meant investigating stakeholder needs and determining which development plan would result in the highest stakeholder satisfaction. We summarized our results in a presentation that highlighted stakeholders, our evaluation criteria, our decision making, and a project implementation plan.

This project taught me the importance of making a clear, concise, and concrete presentation. A big part of the engineering design process is solving the problem, but the more important aspect is being able to effectively convey your solution to an audience.
